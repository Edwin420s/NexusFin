            RESPONSIBLE INNOVATION

            Why Alternative Data & Why Now?

            Some informal workers, gig workers, and small businesses have limited formal financial histories, making conventional credit assessment difficult. Consented transaction data helps verify real cash-flow capacity without invasive data collection.

            5-Step Decision Support Pipeline:

            1. Consented Ingestion: Ingest bank or mobile money transaction export with explicit borrower authorization.
            2. Cash Flow Verification: Reconstruct verified inflows, essential outflows, and net operating cash flow.
            3. Volatility & Debt Detection: Estimate income variability and detect undeclared recurring debt payments.
            4. Deterministic Affordability: Calculate loan repayments, buffers, and stress scenarios using actuarial formulas.
            5. Human Decision Support: Present plain-language trade-offs to the borrower and underwriter for informed human decision.

            Ethical Guardrails Built Into NexusFin:

            • Zero Invasive Scraping: No access to personal contacts, photos, social media, or location tracking.
            • Data Minimization: Only financial transactions, recurring debits, and verified cash flows with explicit consent are analyzed.
            • Cross-Validation: Cross-references declared debt payments against recurring debit transactions.
            • No Black-Box Scoring: Alternative data informs income variability and buffers; it is never converted into an unexplained score.
