Problem Statement 3 • Responsible Credit Decision Support

Understand the credit before it becomes a burden.

NexusFin bridges the gap between credit access and credit affordability. By pairing deterministic cash-flow mathematics, alternative data signals, multi-scenario stress testing, and plain-language explainability, NexusFin empowers borrowers and lenders to prevent over-indebtedness before signing.

💡 Decision Support, Not a Black Box

We never produce an opaque 3-digit score or an automated rejection. NexusFin demonstrates how a proposed repayment behaves under normal conditions and realistic financial shocks (income dips, inflation spikes), keeping the borrower in control of their choice.

            RESPONSIBLE INNOVATION

            Why Alternative Data & Why Now?

            Traditional credit bureau scoring relies on formal bank debt histories and structured payroll pay slips. Across ASEAN, tens of millions of micro-entrepreneurs, gig workers, and informal traders are unrepresented or thin-file, locking them out of fair financing or driving them toward predatory digital lenders.

            Ethical Guardrails Built Into NexusFin:

            • Zero Invasive Scraping: No access to personal phone contact lists, photos, or browsing histories.
            • Data Minimization: Only financial transactions, recurring debits, and verified cash flows with explicit consent are analyzed.
            • Cross-Validation: Automatically cross-references declared debt payments against actual recurring transaction debits.
            • Full Explainability: Alternative data informs income volatility and buffer metrics; it is never converted into an unexplained credit black-box score.
