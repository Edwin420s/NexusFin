            RESPONSIBLE INNOVATION

            Why Alternative Data & Why Now?

            Some informal workers, gig workers, and small businesses have limited formal financial histories, making conventional credit assessment difficult. Consented transaction data helps verify real cash-flow capacity without invasive data collection.

            5-Step Decision Support Pipeline:

            1. Consented Ingestion: Ingest bank or mobile money transaction export with explicit borrower authorization.
            2. Cash Flow Verification: Reconstruct verified inflows, essential outflows, and net operating cash flow.
            3. Volatility & Debt Detection: Estimate income variability and detect undeclared recurring debt payments.
            4. Deterministic Affordability: Calculate loan repayments, buffers, and stress scenarios using actuarial formulas.
            5. Human Decision Support: Present plain-language trade-offs to the borrower and underwriter for informed human decision.

            Ethical Guardrails Built Into NexusFin:

            • Zero Invasive Scraping: No access to personal contacts, photos, social media, or location tracking.
            • Data Minimization: Only financial transactions, recurring debits, and verified cash flows with explicit consent are analyzed.
            • Cross-Validation: Cross-references declared debt payments against recurring debit transactions.
            • No Black-Box Scoring: Alternative data informs income variability and buffers; it is never converted into an unexplained score.

            ACTUARIAL FORMULATIONS

            Core Financial Calculations & Amortization Mechanics

            Transparent formulas governing loan instalments, true APRs, disposable buffers, and debt burden ratios.

            1. Reducing-Balance Instalment (PMT)
            PMT = P × [ r(1 + r)n ] / [ (1 + r)n - 1 ]

            P: Principal borrowed • r: Monthly interest rate (annualRate / 12) • n: Tenure in months.
            Standard amortization: each payment reduces the remaining principal balance, lowering the interest incurred in subsequent periods.

            2. Flat-Rate vs True APR Distortion
            Total Flat Interest = P × rflat × n
            True Effective APR ≈ 1.85 × Flat Rate

            Flat rates charge interest on the entire original principal for the full tenure, even when half the loan has been repaid. NexusFin exposes true effective APR to prevent marketing deception.

            3. Net Post-Loan Cash Buffer
            Buffer = Income - Essential Expenses - Existing Debt - PMT

            Measures actual uncommitted cash remaining after living essentials and all debt service. A positive buffer ensures basic survival without borrowing to service obligations.

            4. Debt-Service Burden (DSB / DTI)
            DSB (%) = [ (Existing Debt + PMT) / Income ] × 100%

            < 30%: Sustainable • 30% – 45%: Caution / Tight • > 45%: High Risk of Over-Indebtedness.
            Aligned with international responsible lending guidelines and debt ceilings.

            ALTERNATIVE DATA ENGINE

            Ethical Cash-Flow Analytics

            Supporting underserved and thin-file borrowers with consented transaction turnover.

            • Zero Contact Scraping: NexusFin strictly rejects accessing contacts, call logs, device storage, photos, or social media data.
            • Financial Turnover Only: Ingests consented transaction history from bank accounts, mobile money (M-Pesa), and merchant platforms (Grab, Foodpanda).
            • Volatility Modeling: Computes the standard coefficient of variation (CV = σ / μ × 100%) to capture irregular earning cycles.
            • Recurring Debt Detection: Algorithmic pattern mining identifies recurring debits, cross-checking borrower self-declarations.
            RESPONSIBLE DATA GOVERNANCE

            Privacy by Design & Consumer Sovereignty

            Rigorous borrower data governance built into every layer of the architecture.

            • Granular Consent: Separate opt-in permissions for transaction ingestion, utility verification, and underwriting sharing.
            • Purpose Limitation: Consented financial data is processed exclusively for affordability evaluation and never sold or shared.
            • Instant Revocation: Borrowers can revoke any data consent toggle instantly with immediate audit logging.
            • Cryptographic Audit Trail: Every data ingestion, consent state update, and underwriter review is logged in an immutable event stream.
