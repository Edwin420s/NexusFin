FINANCIAL LITERACY

The Hidden Trap of Flat-Rate Interest

Many non-bank lenders advertise low monthly flat rates (e.g. "Only 1.5% per month!"). However, flat rate interest calculates interest on the entire original principal for every single month, even when half the loan has already been paid off.

Rule of Thumb: A 15% flat rate is actually equivalent to approximately 27.7% true APR on a reducing-balance schedule. NexusFin recalculates all products to true APR so you can make informed decisions.
BORROWER RIGHTS

Key Facts Statement (KFS) Standards

  • Standardized APR: Always compare products using Effective Annual Interest Rate including all upfront and recurring fees.
  • Total Financing Cost: Compare the total sum of money you pay back above the principal borrowed.
  • Disposable Buffer: Verify that monthly repayments leave at least a 15–20% cushion for food, health, and emergencies.
  • Prepayment Rights: Inquire whether early settlement incurs fees or waives future unaccrued interest charges.
