# NexusFin — 3-Minute Product Walkthrough Guide & Demo Script
**Responsible Credit Decision Support & Affordability Intelligence Platform**
*Target Duration:* 2 minutes 50 seconds to 3 minutes 00 seconds.

---

### [0:00 – 0:25] Introduction & The Problem
**Visual:** Screen showing the NexusFin application header and hero banner: *"Understand credit before it becomes a burden."*
**Script:**
> "Welcome to **NexusFin**.
> Across emerging and digital credit markets, access to loans has never been easier. But credit access is not the same as credit affordability.
> Millions of gig workers, freelancers, and small business owners can qualify for instant credit on their phones, but have no independent way to test whether repayments remain manageable when income drops or expenses spike.
> That is the challenge NexusFin solves: independent decision support before commitments are made."

---

### [0:25 – 1:15] Tab 1: Affordability Engine & Shock Stress Testing
**Visual:** Screen recording of Tab 1. Select the *"Delivery & Logistics Contractor (PHP)"* sample profile. Click *"⚡ Assess Affordability & Resilience"*.
**Script:**
> "Let’s take Carlos, a delivery contractor earning about ₱38,000 a month with 25% income variability. He is evaluating a ₱35,000 microloan over 10 months.
> With one click, NexusFin computes the exact deterministic cash-flow impact.
> Notice our actuarial approach: planned goal savings is tracked as an emergency buffer, never penalized as a debt liability.
> In the baseline case, Carlos has a positive post-loan buffer of ₱8,454, with a 22.5% debt burden. But notice our status conclusion: *Manageable today, vulnerable under income shock*.
> Why? Because life is unpredictable. Look at the **Stress Testing Table.**
> If Carlos faces a 25% drop in platform orders, his monthly income drops to ₱28,500 while fixed living costs remain. With the loan payment, his cash flow enters an immediate −₱1,046 monthly deficit.
> Instead of an opaque 3-digit score, NexusFin explains the exact trade-offs in plain language, helping the borrower negotiate safer terms or adjust repayment horizons."

---

### [1:15 – 1:55] Tab 2: Informed Choice & Comparative Key Facts
**Visual:** Click Tab 2: *"02 Compare Credit Offers"*. Click *"📊 Generate Key Facts Comparison"*. Show the side-by-side comparison matrix.
**Script:**
> "Now let's examine **Informed Choice**.
> Borrowers are frequently marketed loans using flat monthly rates that obscure the true cost of borrowing.
> In Tab 2, NexusFin generates a standardized **Key Facts Statement** comparing competing offers side by side against the borrower's actual cash flow.
> Carlos can immediately see that an offer quoting a low flat rate actually charges significantly more total financing cost than an amortizing community loan.
> The platform flags the lowest total cost, the lowest monthly payment, and the most resilient option, empowering transparent financial choices."

---

### [1:55 – 2:30] Tab 3 & 4: Alternative Data & Privacy by Design
**Visual:** Click Tab 3: *"03 Alternative Data & Cash Flow"*. Click a sample statement pill. Show transaction categorization and the *"Import Detected Cash Flows"* button. Then switch to Tab 4: *"04 Responsible Data Governance & Audit Trail"*. Toggle a consent switch and show the live audit trail updating.
**Script:**
> "For thin-file borrowers without formal credit bureau records, Tab 3 provides a **Consented Alternative Data Engine**.
> By uploading a transaction statement from bank accounts or digital wallets, NexusFin automatically categorizes cash flows, models income volatility, and detects recurring debt payments — with zero intrusive phone scraping or contact access.
> In Tab 4, NexusFin demonstrates **Privacy by Design**: granular, revocable consent controls for every data source and an immutable audit log recording all data operations."

---

### [2:30 – 3:00] Tab 5: Institutional Underwriting & Multi-Currency Architecture
**Visual:** Switch to *"Lender & Underwriter Portal"* via the top mode toggle. Show the underwriter review queue and institutional metrics.
**Script:**
> "Finally, in Tab 5, we provide the **Institutional Underwriter Portal**.
> Lenders, microfinance organizations, and community credit cooperatives can integrate NexusFin via RESTful APIs to assess thin-file applicants responsibly, stress-test borrower cohorts, and prevent over-indebtedness before loan disbursement.
> NexusFin is containerized, pre-calibrated across major currencies (PHP, KES, SGD, IDR, MYR, THB, VND, USD), and ready for enterprise integration.
> NexusFin: turning credit access into genuine financial resilience.
> Thank you."
